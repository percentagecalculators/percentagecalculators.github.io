# Thai (th) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Register: neutral, polite written Thai (ภาษาเขียนทั่วไป); no ครับ/ค่ะ; the reader is addressed as `คุณ` only when needed and often omitted. Headings are noun phrases or questions with `ไหม`/`อย่างไร`, no sentence-final period.
- No spaces between words; a space separates sentences/clauses and Latin or number tokens from Thai where the first-pass pages already do so. Thai has no capitalisation.
- Numbers, decimal point, thousands comma and `$` stay as in English with Western digits (no Thai numerals): 12.5%, $1,250.
- Percent: `20%` attached; prose `เปอร์เซ็นต์`.
- Quotation marks: “…”.
- Latin acronyms kept and glossed (APR, APY, GPA, CGPA, SGPA, ppm); established loanwords in Thai script (คอมมิชชัน, ทิป, มาร์กอัป).
- Reuse first-pass nav names and h1s (เครื่องคำนวณ …).
