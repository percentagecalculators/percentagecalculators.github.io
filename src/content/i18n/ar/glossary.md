# Arabic (ar) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Variety: Modern Standard Arabic, neutral across regions (no dialect). Prefer terms common to Gulf/Levant/Egypt finance and schooling.
- Address: impersonal or masculine-generic imperative/second person ("أدخل"، "اضرب"، "يمكنك"), formal. Headings in plain sentence form; question headings end with `؟`.
- Digits: Western digits (0-9), decimal point `.`, thousands comma `,` and `$` before the number stay exactly as in English ($1,250, 12.5%) — they match the calculator output and the validator. Never Arabic-Indic digits, never `٫` or `٬`.
- Percent: `%` attached after the number (20%). Prose: «النسبة المئوية».
- Punctuation: Arabic comma `،`, semicolon `؛` and question mark `؟` in prose; guillemets «…» for quotation. Not inside formulas/LaTeX.
- Latin acronyms (APR, APY, GPA, CGPA, SGPA, ABV, ppm, COGS, ROI, EBITDA) stay in Latin letters, glossed in Arabic at first use. Page is RTL; formulas, numbers with operators and `<output>` render LTR automatically.
- Reuse the first-pass nav names and h1s (حاسبة …).
