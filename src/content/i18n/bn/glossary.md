# Bengali (bn) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Variety: standard written Bengali (সাধু-free, চলিত ভাষা), neutral between Bangladesh and West Bengal; prefer terms understood in both, with the English term in Latin letters where Bengali readers commonly use it (মার্জিন, মার্কআপ, ক্যালকুলেটর, টিপ).
- Address: polite `আপনি`; verb forms `করুন`, `করতে পারেন`, impersonal where possible. Headings in plain sentence form; question headings end with `?`.
- Digits: Western digits (0-9), decimal point `.`, thousands comma `,` and `$` before the number stay as in English ($1,250, 12.5%); never Bengali digits (০-৯). First-pass Bengali digits in the GPA/CGPA/SGPA and pages files were converted.
- Percent: `%` attached after the number (20%); prose «শতাংশ».
- Punctuation: Bengali danda `।` ends sentences; quotes “…”.
- Latin acronyms (APR, APY, GPA, CGPA, SGPA, ABV, ppm, COGS, ROI, EBITDA) stay in Latin letters, glossed in Bengali at first use.
- Reuse the first-pass nav names and h1s.
