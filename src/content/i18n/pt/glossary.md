# Portuguese (pt) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Variety: Brazilian Portuguese (largest audience; matches the first-pass nav: porcentagem, seu, gorjeta). Use the spelling of the 1990 Orthographic Agreement.
- Address: `você`/`seu`, impersonal where possible ("Para calcular…", "divide-se…" avoided in favour of "divida"/infinitive). Headings in sentence case; question headings end with `?`.
- Numbers, decimal point, thousands comma and `$` stay as in English (they match the calculator output and the validator): 12.5%, $1,250, never 12,5 or 1.250. `$` goes before the number.
- Percent: attached, `20%`. Prose: "porcentagem" (Brazil), "por cento" in spoken-style phrases.
- Quotation marks: “…”.
- Reuse the first-pass nav names and h1s (Calculadora de …).
