# Indonesian (id) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Variety: standard Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia baku, PUEBI), not colloquial; common English loanwords kept where Indonesians use them (markup, margin, tip, kalkulator, diskon).
- Address: `Anda` (capitalised), impersonal where possible. Headings in sentence case; question headings end with `?`.
- Numbers, decimal point, thousands comma and `$` stay as in English (they match the calculator output and the validator): 12.5%, $1,250, never 12,5 or 1.250. `$` goes before the number. First-pass decimal commas in the GPA/CGPA/SGPA texts were fixed.
- Percent: `20%` attached (usual in Indonesian); prose "persen"/"persentase".
- Quotation marks: “…”.
- Reuse the first-pass nav names and h1s (Kalkulator …).
