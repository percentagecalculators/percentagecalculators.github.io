# Korean (ko) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Register: formal polite `합니다체` (-ㅂ니다/-습니다) throughout; instructions as `~하세요` only inside short imperatives such as tips/asides is avoided; prefer `~합니다`, `~할 수 있습니다`, `~하면 됩니다`.
- Script: Hangul, with Latin acronyms; Sino-Korean financial terms where standard (이자, 원금, 복리, 단리, 감가상각, 매출총이익률), katakana-style loanwords as Korean usage has them (퍼센트, 마진, 마크업, 팁, 대출).
- Spacing (띄어쓰기): follow standard rules; particles attach to the preceding word; units/percent attach to numbers (`20%`, `5년`, `$1,250`). No space before 퍼센트 when `%` is used.
- Punctuation: ASCII `. , ? !` as in modern Korean web text; quotes “…”/‘…’.
- Digits: Western digits (0-9), decimal point `.`, thousands comma `,` and `$` before the number stay exactly as English ($1,250, 12.5%). Never 만/억 grouping, never Sino-Korean numerals where English has digits. Where English spells a number as a word (one, two), a native Korean count word is fine when no digit appears (한 개, 두 번), because the digit check compares digit sequences.
- Latin acronyms (APR, APY, GPA, CGPA, SGPA, ABV, ppm, COGS, ROI, EBITDA) stay in Latin letters, glossed in Korean at first use.
- Headings: noun phrases or `~란?` / `~하는 방법`, no trailing period.
- Reuse the first-pass nav names and h1s (…계산기).
