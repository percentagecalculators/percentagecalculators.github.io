# Hindi (hi) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Variety: standard Hindi as used in Indian education and finance, with widely used English loanwords in Devanagari (कैलकुलेटर, मार्जिन, मार्कअप, टिप, लोन, फूड कॉस्ट) rather than coined Sanskritised terms. Hindi is the language of an Indian audience, so Indian examples (CBSE, CGPA ×9.5, ₹ if present in English) stay as in English.
- Address: respectful `आप` with `करें`/`कर सकते हैं`, impersonal where possible. Headings in plain sentence form; question headings end with `?`.
- Digits: Western digits (0-9), decimal point `.`, thousands comma `,` and `$` before the number stay as in English ($1,250, 12.5%); never Devanagari digits (०-९). Lakh/crore grouping is not used.
- Percent: `%` attached (20%); prose «प्रतिशत».
- Punctuation: danda `।` ends sentences; quotes “…”.
- Latin acronyms (APR, APY, GPA, CGPA, SGPA, ABV, ppm, COGS, ROI, EBITDA) stay in Latin letters, glossed in Hindi at first use.
- Reuse the first-pass nav names and h1s.
