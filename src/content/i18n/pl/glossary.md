# Polish (pl) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (first pass):
- Register: neutral, instructional Polish; no "Pan/Pani". Prefer impersonal wording and infinitives ("Aby obliczyć…", "Wpisz…"); address the reader as "Ty" (lowercase in running text, "Twój" only when needed). Headings in sentence case; question headings end with `?`.
- Numbers, decimal point, thousands comma and `$` stay as in English (they match the calculator output): 12.5%, $1,250.00, never 12,5 or 1 250. `$` goes before the number.
- Percent: attached to the digits, `20%` (no space). In prose: "procent" (e.g. "20 procent" only when spelled out).
- Quotation marks: „…”.
- Tool names follow the form users search for: "Kalkulator procentowy", "Kalkulator procentów" as a natural variant; pattern "Kalkulator …" for every tool.
- Static pages: "O nas", "Kontakt", "Mapa strony", "Zastrzeżenia prawne", "Polityka prywatności", "Regulamin".
