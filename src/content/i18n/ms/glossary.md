# Malay (ms) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Variety: standard Malaysian Malay (Bahasa Melayu baku, DBP spelling: `baharu`, `peratusan`/`peratus`), not Indonesian vocabulary (faedah not bunga, perbelanjaan not pengeluaran, kos not biaya, markah not nilai for marks).
- Address: `anda` (lower case), impersonal where possible. Headings in sentence case; question headings end with `?`.
- Numbers, decimal point, thousands comma and `$` stay as in English (they match the calculator output and the validator): 12.5%, $1,250. `$` before the number.
- Percent: `20%` attached; prose `peratus`/`peratusan`.
- Quotation marks: “…”.
- Reuse the first-pass nav names and h1s (Kalkulator …).
