# Dutch (nl) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Variety: standard Netherlands Dutch (Dutch spelling, 2015 Woordenlijst), no Flemish-specific vocabulary.
- Address: `je`/`jouw` (informal, as in the first-pass cards), not `u`. Headings in sentence case (Dutch convention); question headings end with `?`.
- Numbers, decimal point, thousands comma and `$` stay as in English (they match the calculator output and the validator): 12.5%, $1,250. The first-pass `4,0`/`9,5` in the GPA/CGPA/SGPA files were corrected to `4.0`/`9.5`.
- Percent: `20%` attached; prose `procent`/`percentage`.
- Quotation marks: „…” avoided; use “…”.
- Latin acronyms kept (APR, APY, GPA, CGPA, SGPA, COGS→ "inkoopwaarde omzet (COGS)").
- Reuse first-pass nav names and h1s (… Calculator).

## Open for review (self-review by Claude, no native reviewer)

- `je` vs `u`: chosen `je` for consistency with the cards; a formal finance audience might prefer `u`.
- Indian CGPA class labels ("Eerste klasse met onderscheiding", "Voldoende") are own renderings, not official.
- Distilling terms (voorloop/hart/nalopen, kolomstill) not checked with a native distiller.
- SGPA article follows English (SGPA × 10) − 7.5; the subtitle says × 9.5 (inconsistency in the English source).
- Loanwords/sales jargon kept in English: clawback, SPIFF, OTE, target.
