# Vietnamese (vi) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Address: `bạn` (neutral, friendly), imperative `Hãy …`/`Nhập …` as in the first-pass cards. Headings in sentence case; question headings end with `?`.
- Numbers, decimal point, thousands comma and `$` stay as in English with Western digits (they match the calculator output and the validator): 12.5%, $1,250. First-pass `4,0`/`9,5` in the GPA/CGPA/SGPA files were corrected to `4.0`/`9.5`.
- Percent: `20%` attached; prose `phần trăm`.
- Quotation marks: “…”.
- Latin acronyms kept and glossed (APR, APY, GPA, CGPA, SGPA, ppm); finance loanwords used as in the first-pass nav (markup).
- Reuse first-pass nav names and h1s (Máy tính …). Vietnamese separates syllables with spaces, so no special segmentation rule.

## Open for review (self-review by Claude, no native reviewer)

- `bạn` register in finance articles; markup/proof loanwords kept in Latin form.
