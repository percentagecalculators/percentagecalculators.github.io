# Japanese (ja) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Register: です・ます体 throughout (polite, neutral); no だ・である. Instructions as 「〜します」「〜してください」, not commands.
- Script: kanji + hiragana, katakana for loanwords per usual business Japanese (パーセント, マージン, ローン, チップ). Prefer established Japanese financial terms (利率, 元本, 複利, 減価償却, 売上総利益率) over katakana where they exist.
- Punctuation: full-width 、。 and 「」 for quotes; `?` in FAQ questions written as `？` (full-width) — digits and `%` stay half-width. Parentheses （） full-width around Japanese, ( ) is fine around Latin/digits only. No spaces between Japanese and Latin letters/digits.
- Digits: Western digits (0-9), decimal point `.`, thousands comma `,` and `$` before the number stay exactly as in English ($1,250, 12.5%). Never full-width digits, never 万/億 grouping, never `ドル` instead of `$`.
- Percent: `20%` attached (usual); prose 「パーセント」/「割合」. Do not use 割・分 notation (3割) because digits must match English.
- Latin acronyms (APR, APY, GPA, CGPA, SGPA, ABV, ppm, COGS, ROI, EBITDA) stay in Latin letters, glossed in Japanese at first use.
- Headings: noun phrases or 「〜とは？」/「〜の方法」, no trailing 。.
- Reuse the first-pass nav names and h1s (…計算機).
