# Italian (it) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Variety: standard Italian (Italy). Address: impersonal where possible, otherwise formal `Lei` (verb forms `calcoli`, `inserisca` avoided; prefer infinitives and "si" constructions: "Per calcolare…", "Si divide…"). Headings in Italian sentence case (only first word and proper nouns capitalised); question headings end with `?`.
- Numbers, decimal point, thousands comma and `$` stay as in English (they match the calculator output and the validator): 12.5%, $1,250, never 12,5 or 1.250. `$` goes before the number.
- Percent: attached, `20%` (usual in running Italian); "per cento" in prose where natural.
- Quotation marks: «…».
- Reuse the first-pass nav names and h1s (Calcolatore di …).
