# French (fr) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Variety: standard international French (France as the default, no Quebec-only forms): pourboire, remise, coût, bénéfice, taux.
- Address: `vous`, impersonal where possible. Headings in sentence case.
- Numbers, decimal point, thousands comma and `$` stay as in English (they match the calculator output and the validator); French decimal commas are NOT used in articles.
- Typography is applied automatically by `add_content.py` (write plain spaces): non-breaking space before `: ; ? ! %` and inside guillemets « ». Use « » for quotation marks, never "...".
- Percent: `20 %` (with the non-breaking space); "pour cent" in running text.
- Reuse the first-pass names: the nav names and h1s (calculateur, "en pourcentage").
