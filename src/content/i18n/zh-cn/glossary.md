# Simplified Chinese (zh-cn) glossary for articles and FAQs

Decisions (made 2026-10-28):
- Variety: Mainland standard written Chinese (简体, Mainland terminology: 计算器, 软件, 信息, 默认, 利息/利率, 税务, 毛利率).
- Address: `您` as in the first-pass cards, used sparingly (often omitted). Headings are noun phrases or questions ending with `？`; no sentence-final period on headings.
- Punctuation: full-width `，。、；：？！（）` and quotes `“…”`/`‘…’`. Inside LaTeX and next to digits/Latin acronyms the usual half-width characters stay. A decimal point stays `.`.
- Numbers, decimal point, thousands comma and `$` stay as in English with Western digits (they match the calculator output and the validator): 12.5%, $1,250. Do not use 万/亿, 折, 成 notation (e.g. write “八折” only if the English has no digits; otherwise “20% 折扣”).
- Percent: `20%` attached to the number; prose `百分比`/`百分之`.
- Spacing: one space between Latin acronyms/words and Chinese (`APR 转 APY`, `GPA 成绩`), as in the first-pass names; none between digits and Chinese measure words or `%`.
- Latin acronyms kept and glossed (APR, APY, GPA, CGPA, SGPA, ppm).
- Reuse first-pass nav names and h1s (… 计算器).
