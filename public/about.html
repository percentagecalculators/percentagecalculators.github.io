About Percentage Calculators

Our Mission

Percentage Calculators was created with a simple goal: to make percentage calculations fast, accurate, and accessible to everyone. Whether you're a student working on homework, a professional analyzing business metrics, or someone calculating a tip at a restaurant – we've got you covered.

Who We Are

Hi! I'm Subash Geetha Krishnan, a Tech Lead with over 15 years of experience in enterprise technology. Based in Singapore, I've spent my career solving complex technical problems and building tools that make life easier.

After years of searching for simple, reliable percentage calculators online and finding most options cluttered with ads or lacking features, I decided to build something better. Percentage Calculators is the result – a clean, fast, and completely free suite of tools.

What We Offer

Our platform includes 10 specialized percentage calculators, each designed for specific use cases:

  • Percentage Increase Calculator – Add percentages to numbers for raises, markups, and growth
  • Percentage Decrease Calculator – Subtract percentages for discounts, depreciation, and reductions
  • Percentage Change Calculator – Find the change between two values over time
  • Percentage Difference Calculator – Compare two numbers symmetrically
  • Percentage Off Calculator – Calculate sale prices and savings while shopping
  • Fraction to Percentage Calculator – Convert fractions and test scores to percentages
  • Percentage Growth Calculator – Track business metrics and KPIs
  • Reverse Percentage Calculator – Find original numbers from percentages
  • Percentage Error Calculator – Calculate experimental accuracy for lab work
  • Average Percentage Calculator – Find the mean of multiple percentages

Why Choose Us?

  • 100% Free – All tools are completely free with no registration required
  • Instant Results – Calculations happen as you type, no buttons to click
  • Mobile Friendly – Works perfectly on any device
  • No Ads Overload – Clean interface focused on functionality
  • Privacy First – We don't track your calculations or sell your data
  • Formula Display – See the math behind every calculation to learn as you go

Our Commitment
