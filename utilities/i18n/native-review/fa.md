## Style decisions to confirm
- Western digits (0-9) kept on purpose so the text matches the calculator output; Persian digits (۰-۹) are the alternative.
- Calculator name written spaced («ماشین حساب») in titles; ZWNJ form («ماشین‌حساب») acceptable in prose. Which is preferred?
- LaTeX labels spell «درصد» instead of keeping `\%` (other languages keep `\%`).
- Register: formal-polite (شما / impersonal).
